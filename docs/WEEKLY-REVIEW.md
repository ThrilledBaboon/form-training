# Optional weekly review

Add Weekly review through Edit page → Add widget, or open the Weekly review page. It is not inserted into existing or default pages automatically. Choose a day in the week to review; the current week stops at today, while future weeks have no review yet.

The review shows only enabled systems: completed Gym and Climbing sessions, distinct activity days, recorded duration with unknown durations counted separately, working sets, climbing attempts/clean completions and food logging coverage. Previous-week activity compares equivalent days for a partial week. Food averages use only known daily totals on days with food logged; incomplete totals and unlogged days stay visible as missing. No calorie field is introduced.

Each section links to source history or diary records. The review does not produce a health score, infer recovery, treat food logging as complete nutrition, or claim food caused a performance change. Widget settings travel in shared layouts; personal records do not.
