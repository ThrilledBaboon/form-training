# Suggest a meal

Open Suggest a meal from Food home, Food navigation, or an optional widget. It sorts active, complete-menu recipes using their recorded ingredients, nutrition, kitchen stock, preparation/cooking time and purchase prices. Each option shows its reasons, one-serving macros and any shortages.

Choose balanced, remaining macros, use stock/expiry, quickest or lowest recorded cost. Remaining goals compare saved gram targets against today's logged food; planned meals are not counted as eaten. Missing macro values remain unknown. Zero prep/cook time is treated as unrecorded, and unknown prices never win as if they were free. A time/cost limit excludes options without a known value.

Review a recipe before choosing Plan or Log. These open the existing editors; ranking does not create meals, change goals or deduct stock. Calories remain hidden unless the user has explicitly enabled them in Food display. Suggestions use no external service or health data.
